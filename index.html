Docs and FAQ

What is Vela?
Vela is a staking and token launchpad on Robinhood Chain. You can stake VELA for rewards, launch a token in one transaction, and trade it from your own wallet.
Which wallets work?
Any EVM wallet, such as MetaMask or Trust Wallet. Open Vela in your wallet’s browser and tap Connect wallet. Robinhood Chain (chain ID 4663) is added for you.
How does staking work?
Pick a pool (Flex, 30 days or 90 days), enter an amount and confirm. The APR is locked when you stake. When the lock ends, tap Unstake to receive your VELA plus rewards.
How does a launch work?
Every token has a fixed supply of 1 billion. 800 million are sold on a bonding curve and 200 million are kept for liquidity. The price rises as people buy and falls as they sell.
What is graduation?
When all 800 million curve tokens are sold, at about a $42,000 market cap, the token moves to the Vela DEX automatically. The raised USDG and the 200 million tokens become the first liquidity, and that liquidity is locked.
What are the fees?
On the curve the fee is 1% (0.60% to the creator, 0.40% to the Vela treasury). After graduation the fee is 1%: 0.60% creator, 0.30% liquidity providers and 0.10% treasury. Tokens launched elsewhere pay only the 0.30% liquidity fee. Fees are shown before you confirm.
Can I sell before a token graduates?
Yes. You can sell back to the curve at any time. Until a token graduates it can only be traded with the curve, not sent between wallets.
How do creators earn?
Creators earn 0.60% of every USDG trade in their token. Open Launch and use Creator earnings to claim it in USDG.
How do liquidity providers earn?
Providers earn 0.30% of every trade in the pool, paid in USDG. Open Trade, load a graduated token and tap Claim fees at any time.
Where can I verify the contracts?
Every Vela contract is listed on the About page with a link to the Robinhood Chain explorer.

About Vela

Vela is a staking and token launchpad on Robinhood Chain. Stake VELA to earn APR rewards, launch a fixed-supply token in one transaction, and trade it against USDG. Creators earn 0.60% of every trade in their token, and everything runs from your own wallet. Vela never holds your funds.

How Vela works

1. Launch
Create a token with a fixed 1 billion supply. It trades on a bonding curve from the first block.
2. Graduate
At about $42k market cap the token moves to the Vela DEX automatically, and its liquidity is locked.
3. Earn
Creators earn 0.60% of every trade in their token. Stake VELA to earn APR rewards.
